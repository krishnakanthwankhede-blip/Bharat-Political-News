About Bharat Political News

Bharat Political News is an independent digital news initiative focused on Indian politics, Karnataka, Parliament, elections and public-interest issues. Our aim is to make political information easier to understand and verify.

Editorial principles

Distinguish fact, allegation and opinion. Attribute statements. Link primary sources where possible. Correct material errors transparently. Do not publish unverified claims as fact.
